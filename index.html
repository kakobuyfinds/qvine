From discovery to decision

How to use a LoveGoBuy spreadsheet

A LoveGoBuy spreadsheet helps you organize potential purchases and compare source listings. Treat each find as a starting point: the exact seller link, chosen variant and current buying terms matter more than a product title alone.

01

Find and identify

Start with a category or product type. On HipobuyFind, locate the item by its name or ID and inspect the source listing. Record the seller link, color, size and variant so similar products do not get mixed up.

02

Check before paying

Compare measurements with an item you own. Review seller details, current stock and return conditions. If buying through LoveGoBuy, confirm that the exact link is supported and review the current order quote in your account.

03

Review the parcel

When warehouse photos are available, inspect the received size, color, quantity and visible condition. Resolve concerns within the applicable return window, then compare shipping options for your actual destination.

Look beyond the product photo.

Fit and listing checklist

  • Match the exact size chart to the selected product and variant.
  • For clothing, compare chest, length, shoulders and sleeves with a garment laid flat.
  • For shoes, check the seller’s measurement method and sizing notes.
  • For bags, check dimensions, closures, straps and included accessories.
  • Keep the item link and variant details with your shortlist.

QC and parcel checklist

  • Seller photography shows the advertised product; it is not proof of what will arrive.
  • Other buyers’ QC photos can provide context but may show a different batch.
  • Check photos of your received item when offered by your purchasing service.
  • Confirm return deadlines before approving onward shipping.
  • Use the packed parcel’s quoted weight and dimensions for shipping decisions.
Useful answers

LoveGoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a LoveGoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product discovery list used to organize finds and source links for potential purchases through LoveGoBuy. This page presents a small searchable selection from HipobuyFind alongside buying guidance, rather than a downloadable spreadsheet.

Where do the product buttons take me?

Every product button and both main call-to-action buttons open https://hipobuyfind.com. Find the matching item there using the displayed name or item ID, inspect its details, and review available purchasing links.

Are these live prices or guaranteed stock?

No. These CNY item prices were retrieved from HipobuyFind on November 19, 2026. This page does not synchronize inventory or prices. Confirm the exact variant, current price and availability before placing an order.

Do these prices include delivery?

The displayed figures are source-listed item prices. Domestic delivery, international shipping, service charges, insurance and applicable destination charges may add to the total. Obtain current quotes for your order and parcel.

Have these products been independently tested?

No. Inclusion confirms that the listing appeared in the source catalog, not that the product was tested. This page does not certify authenticity, quality, seller reliability or suitability. Review the listing and available evidence yourself.

Is this the official LoveGoBuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery guide with no claimed official partnership. It does not take payments, manage orders or provide warehouse services. Product discovery buttons lead to HipobuyFind.
